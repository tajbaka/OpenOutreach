# Boundera Personas

## CSP / Security Operator

Typical titles: CISO, CTO, VP Security, Head of GRC, Cloud Security, Compliance Lead, FedRAMP Program Manager.

Pain:
- Manual evidence collection from AWS, Azure, GCP, IdP, ticketing, and docs
- Keeping controls current after initial authorization
- Coordinating engineering remediation
- Preparing SSP, KSI, POA&M, and evidence packages

Message angle:
> Boundera connects to cloud and security systems, identifies evidence and gaps, tracks remediation, and keeps the authorization package current.

Best ask:
> Is evidence, remediation ownership, or ongoing monitoring the bigger drag for your team right now?

## Advisor / vCISO / Consultant

Typical titles: vCISO, GRC advisor, FedRAMP consultant, security program advisor, compliance leader.

Pain:
- Delivery leverage across clients
- Reducing spreadsheet and screenshot work
- Turning expertise into repeatable workflows
- Better client visibility and evidence traceability

Message angle:
> Boundera gives advisors a repeatable way to help clients move through FedRAMP readiness, evidence review, remediation, and continuous monitoring.

Best ask:
> Would this be more useful as an internal delivery tool, a client recommendation, or not relevant to how you work?

## 3PAO / Assessor

Typical titles: assessor, audit lead, FedRAMP practice leader, compliance assessor, GRC auditor.

Pain:
- Reviewing scattered evidence
- Traceability from control/KSI to evidence
- Assessing live evidence vs static screenshots
- Generating or validating assessor-facing outputs

Message angle:
> Boundera aims to make evidence easier to review and trace back to live source systems, with assessor-facing workflows.

Best ask:
> Where does assessor review break down most often: evidence quality, traceability, remediation tracking, or package completeness?

## Channel / Distributor / Partner

Typical companies: Carahsoft, Red River, SHI, WWT, DLT, federal resellers and marketplace partners.

Pain:
- Vendors in ecosystem need FedRAMP help
- Partners need a clear fit before routing internally
- People may not personally own FedRAMP, but can forward to partner/public-sector/security teams

Message angle:
> Boundera may be relevant to vendors in your ecosystem preparing for or progressing through FedRAMP.

Best ask:
> Who is the right person or team to speak with about FedRAMP tooling for vendors?

## Federal SI / MSP / Delivery Partner

Typical titles: cloud/AI solutions, managed services, federal delivery, program director, solutions architect.

Pain:
- Customer cloud/security delivery
- Federal modernization work with compliance constraints
- Operationalizing evidence and remediation across teams

Message angle:
> Boundera can support federal cloud/security projects where FedRAMP evidence, remediation, and continuous monitoring become operational bottlenecks.

Best ask:
> Is this something your cloud/security team would evaluate for federal customers, or is there a better internal owner?
