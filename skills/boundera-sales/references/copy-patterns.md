# Boundera Sales Copy Patterns

Last researched: 2026-08-29.

This reference separates measured findings, Boundera operating budgets, and
platform ceilings. The budgets are house rules informed by the research; they
are not promises that length alone causes a reply.

## Operating Budgets

| Message profile | Target | Structure | Boundera ceiling | Primary CTA |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| Cold email | 50–90 words | 3–4 sentences; 2–4 short paragraphs | 100 words | Interest or useful offer |
| Warm scheduling email | 30–80 words | 2–4 sentences | 120 words | Specific next step or time |
| Email reply or objection | 20–75 words | 1–3 short paragraphs | 100 words | One answer or question |
| Post-meeting recap | 80–150 words | Context plus at most 3–5 bullets | 180 words | Confirmed next action |
| Cold-email bump | 5–25 words | One sentence | 35 words | Simplify existing ask |
| LinkedIn connection note | 120–180 characters | 1–2 sentences | 200 characters | Connect; usually no meeting ask |
| LinkedIn first DM or InMail | 200–400 characters, about 35–65 words | 2–4 short sentences | 500 characters | Easy-response question or offer |
| LinkedIn reply or nudge | 15–50 words | 1–3 sentences | 80 words | One answer or next step |
| SMS or Slack note | 10–45 words | 1–3 sentences | 70 words | One coordination step |

The post-meeting, warm-reply, SMS, and exact minimums are Boundera workflow
choices rather than independently proven performance bands. The cold-email and
LinkedIn upper ranges have the strongest direct evidence.

For email, count the body including the greeting and CTA but excluding the
subject and a fixed sender signature. For LinkedIn, count the entire message.

## Cold Email Pattern

Use four units, normally one sentence each:

1. A verified reason this company or person is relevant now.
2. The buyer's likely priority or problem, stated without pretending it is
   confirmed.
3. One specific Boundera connection or credible proof point.
4. One low-friction offer or interest question.

Do not ask a cold recipient to choose among a demo, one-pager, call, referral,
and product tour. Pick one outcome.

For executives, use a 1–4 word subject and a 50–100 word body. Lead with a
strategic priority and offer a useful view, comparison, or example instead of
asking for `15 minutes` with no value attached.

## Warm Email Pattern

Warm copy can be more direct because the recipient already has context:

1. Acknowledge the actual last interaction.
2. State the one decision or next step.
3. Offer one or two concrete times when scheduling.
4. Add the correct calendar link as a fallback, not as a naked demand.

When the email must be forwarded internally, include enough nouns to travel:
who Boundera is, why it may matter to this account, and the requested next step.
Do not turn `forwardable` into a feature dump.

## LinkedIn Pattern

Connection note:

1. Specific reason to connect.
2. Human, low-pressure close.

Do not pitch features or include a calendar link in the connection note.

First DM or InMail:

1. Context cue or relevant trigger.
2. Recipient-centered problem or priority.
3. One Boundera connection.
4. One easy-response CTA.

A reaction, accepted connection, or generic `thanks` is not permission for an
immediate product monologue. Continue at the level of interest actually shown.

## Subject Lines

Target 1–4 words, approximately 12–40 characters. Use natural sentence case or
lowercase. Good subjects name the priority or shared context; weak subjects try
to sell before the body is opened.

Avoid by default:

- `AI`, `platform`, `all-in-one`, or other category buzzwords.
- ROI promises or numbers without context.
- generic social proof.
- clickbait questions.
- `quick question`, `following up`, or `touching base`.

## Calls to Action

- Cold: ask whether the issue is relevant or offer a specific useful artifact,
  comparison, or perspective.
- Engaged opportunity: ask for the actual next decision and, when appropriate,
  suggest a specific date/time.
- Scheduling: propose one or two times, then offer the canonical booking link as
  a fallback.
- Reply/objection: answer first; ask at most one necessary question.

Exactly one primary CTA is a Boundera house rule. The reviewed research did not
directly isolate one CTA versus several CTAs; the rule exists to remove competing
asks and make replies easy.

## Research Basis

### Email

- Gong analyzed more than 28 million cold emails and reported that the highest
  reply rates came from messages of 100 words or fewer and 3–4 sentences. The
  same analysis reported that overt pitching reduced reply rates by as much as
  57%. Source: [Gong cold-email analysis](https://www.gong.io/blog/does-cold-email-even-work-any-more-heres-what-the-data-says).
- Gong's 85-million-email guide reports its strongest body bucket at 51–100
  words and recommends subject lines under four words. Source:
  [Gong cold-email guide](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-guide-how-to-master-cold-email-get-the-data-backed-guide-based-on-85-million-emails.pdf).
- Gong's executive analysis reports 1–4 word subjects performing best and
  highest-performing executive emails at 50–100 words, with reply rates falling
  sharply above 100 words. Source:
  [Gong executive-email analysis](https://www-vercel.gong.io/blog/do-execs-really-reply-to-cold-email-here-s-what-the-data-says).
- In active opportunities, Gong reports 37% success for a specific date/time CTA
  versus 28% for other CTAs. This finding applies to engaged opportunities, not
  cold first touches. Source:
  [Gong sales-email guide](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-guide-7-laws-of-highly-effective-sales-emails.pdf).

### LinkedIn

- LinkedIn seller data reported that messages of 201–400 characters performed
  16% above average, with performance declining as messages became longer.
  Source: [LinkedIn State of Sales 2021](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/sales-solutions/products/pdfs/LSS_SoS21_051221.pdf).
- LinkedIn's analysis of tens of millions of recruiter InMails found messages
  under 400 characters 22% above the overall response average, while messages
  over 1,200 characters were 11% below it. Individually sent InMails performed
  about 15% better than bulk. This is recruiter data, so use it as directional
  support rather than an exact B2B-sales forecast. Source:
  [LinkedIn InMail analysis](https://www.linkedin.com/business/talent/blog/talent-strategy/these-inmails-get-best-response-rates).
- LinkedIn's seller guidance recommends no more than 500 characters for an
  InMail and reports a 14% response lift for subjects of three words or fewer.
  Source: [LinkedIn sales InMail guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/business/sales/blog/inmail/what-a-strong-linkedin-inmail-template-looks-like).

### Current Platform Ceilings

- LinkedIn currently documents a 200-character connection-note limit for Basic
  accounts and 300 for Premium on one Help page. Because LinkedIn's own pages and
  interfaces can vary, 200 characters is the safe cross-account ceiling and the
  live composer remains authoritative. Source:
  [LinkedIn invitation distinction](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a6239760).
- LinkedIn documents a 200-character InMail subject limit and 1,900-character
  body limit. These are technical limits, not performance targets. Source:
  [LinkedIn InMail limits](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a411986/inmail-character-limits?lang=en).

## Evidence Caveats

- These are observational datasets from vendor and platform populations, not
  randomized experiments. They show useful correlations, not guaranteed causal
  effects.
- Open rates are noisy because privacy tooling and security scanners create
  false opens. Prefer positive replies, qualified conversations, and meetings as
  outcome measures.
- Requested proposals, technical answers, legal language, and substantive
  meeting recaps need enough detail to be correct. Do not amputate necessary
  context to satisfy a cold-outreach budget.
- No credible primary-source finding reviewed here proved that adding a booking
  link increases replies. Use the repo's canonical link as a convenience after
  interest exists, not as an unexplained cold CTA.

## Sending Guardrails

This skill drafts copy; it does not operate sending infrastructure. If copy is
used in outbound automation, keep sender identity, subject, links, and headers
accurate and honor opt-outs. Current references:

- [Gmail sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126?hl=en)
- [FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)

The FTC guidance covers U.S. B2B commercial email. Other jurisdictions may
require stricter consent or disclosure practices.
