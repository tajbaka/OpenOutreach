# Message Patterns

## Copy Rules

- Write like a direct founder, not a sequence.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Ask one question.
- Avoid filler: "just checking in", "circling back", "hope all is well" unless the relationship supports it.
- Do not overuse "AI copilot" if the recipient is confused. Explain the workflow.
- Capitalize FedRAMP correctly.
- Use POA&Ms, SSP, KSIs only when the recipient understands compliance vocabulary.
- Do not create pressure with fake urgency.
- Do not ask permission to send updates when the prospect already said "stay in touch."

## LinkedIn Reply

Structure:
1. Acknowledge their latest message.
2. Clarify or reframe in one sentence.
3. Ask one direct question.

Example:
> Fair point. I am asking for the best person at Carahsoft to speak with about FedRAMP tooling for vendors.
>
> Who owns that?

## Email Follow-Up

Structure:
1. Subject if needed.
2. Context line.
3. Forwardable product explanation.
4. Concrete ask.

Example:
> Hi [Name],
>
> [Referral/context].
>
> Boundera helps software vendors working through FedRAMP reduce manual evidence, POA&M, SSP/KSI package, and continuous monitoring work.
>
> Who is the right person to speak with about technology manufacturer or partner fit?

## Slack Nurture

Structure:
1. Thank them for checking internally.
2. Acknowledge timing.
3. State future nurture without requiring a response.

Example:
> Totally understand. Appreciate you checking with the team.
>
> Sounds like timing is not right for Sidekick right now, which makes sense.
>
> I will keep you lightly in the loop as the sandbox and FedRAMP 20x evidence workflows mature. If a client situation comes up where this becomes relevant, happy to reconnect.

## Call Script

Open:
> Hey [Name], this is Arian with Boundera. [Context: you were chatting with Leili / we connected on LinkedIn / you asked for info]. Do you have 30 seconds?

Reason:
> We are building Boundera to reduce the manual evidence, gap, POA&M, SSP/KSI, and continuous monitoring work around FedRAMP.

Discovery:
> Where do you usually see the biggest breakdown: evidence collection, remediation ownership, assessor review, package generation, or keeping everything current after authorization?

Ask:
> Would it be worth showing you a 10-minute walkthrough and getting your blunt feedback?

Voicemail:
> Hey [Name], Arian with Boundera. [Context]. We are building around FedRAMP evidence, gaps, POA&Ms, SSP/KSI packages, and continuous monitoring. I will send a short note as well. Thanks.

## Account Routing Ask

Use when the recipient is a channel/partner/distributor lead:

> I am trying to find the right owner for FedRAMP tooling or vendor partner fit. Is that you, or is there someone better internally?

## Relationship Nurture

Use after a "not now" with future openness:

> Totally understand. Timing matters.
>
> I will keep you lightly in the loop as the product matures. If a client or vendor situation comes up where FedRAMP automation, evidence review, or continuous monitoring becomes painful, happy to reconnect.
